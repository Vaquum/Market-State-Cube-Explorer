# Manual trend-line operator checklist

Instrument for PRD-0005 / P5-S1. **Human sessions are outstanding.** Automated browser tests and color simulations are engineering evidence; they do not complete this checklist.

Use the intended operator and an independent participant where available. Record the actual sample, device, browser, build SHA, theme and modality. Seek color-vision differences where feasible; record limits when unavailable. Give at most five minutes of onboarding: G enters persistent Trend line mode to draw, move or resize; Pan navigates; right-click a line for Delete line; Lines has visibility checkboxes; Trend toolbar More manages drawings, Coordinates creates an exact line, and Undo recovers a drawing command. Do not demonstrate the case answers.

Serve `node tests/support/cube-fake.js --profile mini --port 8790` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8790/#t=2026-09-23T12:00Z~2026-09-24T12:00Z&p=24600~25400&r=4,0&vis=2`. Begin each case with its stated setup. Start the timer after reading the prompt. Record the first attempt before assistance; keep any rerun as attempt 2 with the reason. Never replace the first result.

The critical cases must succeed on the first attempt in **every exercised route**. Report success per participant and per modality. Missing routes/sessions remain outstanding; no pooled result hides a critical failure. Record completion time, errors, accidental market actions, prompts needed and the participant's explanation. Make no population-wide UX claim from this sample.
